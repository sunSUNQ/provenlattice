## What the frontier does and does not tell you

**Does:** enumerate *shard paths by directory prefix* whose boundary edges touch the changed shards; give the exact aggregate edge count (1031) and frontier size (49); give edge direction (`DEPENDS_ON`); and flag wide impact. These are structural facts and can be cited directly.

**Does not:**
- It is **shard/path-level, not symbol-level** — the envelope states plainly "no symbol-level impact is derivable from this call." It cannot tell you which functions or call sites are affected.
- Membership means **"an edge crosses the shard boundary," not "uses the changed interface."** Shard `db` covers *every* file directly under `db/` (e.g. `db/wal_manager.cc`), so `db/db_impl` appearing could be caused by unrelated `db/*.h` dependencies rather than `builder.cc`.
- It carries **no severity, no behavioral semantics, and no direction-of-use** — a dependent edge may be a direct call or a transitive header include.
- It is **conservative/over-approximate by construction** (1-hop, shard granularity): presence ⇒ "touches", never "genuinely broken". Conversely, **absence is not evidence of no impact** — the listing is budgeted (20 of 49 shards and 20 of 1031 edge ids returned; retry will not expand), so unlisted shards are simply unnamed here.
