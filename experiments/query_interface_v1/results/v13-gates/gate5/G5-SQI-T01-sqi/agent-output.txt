**What it's responsible for:** `DownloadEngine` is the central event-driven driver of aria2's download loop. It owns the `EventPoll` object and a queue of `Command` objects (including "routine commands"), and its `run()` method polls for socket/name-resolver readiness, dispatches the corresponding commands, and repeats until no downloads remain. Alongside the loop it holds the shared manager objects the commands rely on — `RequestGroupMan`, `FileAllocationMan`, `CheckIntegrityMan`, `CookieStorage`, `DNSCache`, and the pooled-socket cache — and exposes halt/force-halt control and socket-pool lifetime management.
