## Responsibility

`DownloadEngine` is aria2's central event-loop driver: its `run()` method repeatedly waits for I/O readiness via an `EventPoll`, then dispatches registered `Command` objects from its `commands_` and `routineCommands_` queues until no work remains (`DownloadEngine.cc:158-185`). It owns and coordinates the shared download machinery — the `RequestGroupMan`, `FileAllocationMan`, `CheckIntegrityMan`, `DNSCache`, `CookieStorage`, the reusable socket pool, and statistics (`DownloadEngine.h:88-172`). It also exposes the socket registration/unregistration and socket-pooling APIs that individual commands use to hook into the poll loop (`DownloadEngine.h:185-277`).
